✦ AGENTS

AI Agents Architecture

Fulfillment Orchestrator

Central coordinator that runs the full monitor cycle: checks delays, evaluates reroutes, predicts failures, and optimizes costs across all agents.

Routing Agent

Selects optimal fulfillment center & carrier rate for new orders based on cost, location, and SLA requirements.

Monitor Agent

Queries active shipments every N seconds, detects delays and overdue status, and triggers alerts.

Rerouting Agent

Evaluates alternative carriers when delays occur, checks feasibility, and executes automated reroutes.

Communication Agent

Sends delay alerts via email (SendGrid) and SMS (Twilio) with customer-preferred channels.

Prediction Agent

Predicts failure probability for shipments based on delay history, carrier statistics, and risk factors using vector similarity.

Cost Optimizer

Analyzes shipping costs per monitor cycle, identifies trends, and generates cost-reduction recommendations.

✦ SAFETY

Business Guardrails

Safety rules enforced during every monitor cycle to ensure business compliance

🛡

SLA Compliance

Flags shipments exceeding critical SLA hours thresholds

💰

Cost Cap

Prevents reroutes exceeding maximum allowed cost increase percentage

🔕

Notification Frequency

Limits notifications per order to avoid spam (max 4)

⚠

Failed Delivery Threshold

Flags shipments exceeding overdue delivery threshold

🔀

Carrier Diversity

Prevents switching to same/similar monopoly carriers (>70% volume)

📍

Address Validation

Validates address format, ZIP code, and street indicators

⚡ All guardrails run asynchronously during each Celery Beat monitor cycle (every 15 minutes)
